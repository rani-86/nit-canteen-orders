## Conversation

**Person:** Kamal Singh ("Canteen Bhaiya")
**Role:** Order-taker at NIT Canteen. He takes orders, packs them, garnishes and calls out names at hand-over. A second person does the cooking.
**When / where:** 17 Nov 2026, between 8:30 and 9 pm, at the counter

### Three things he said (paraphrased)
1. "½" on his pad means a half plate; no mark means a full plate.
2. A circled "P" means the order is a parcel.
3. The work is split between two people: one cooks, and he takes the orders, packs, garnishes and calls out the names.

### One thing that surprised me
His memory. He writes no names on the pad, yet he remembers which order belongs to whom. By my rough estimate, only about 5–10% of orders go wrong, and in the end almost everyone gets the right order. I expected far more mix-ups.

This changed how I see the problem. He isn't constantly failing. The whole system runs on one person's memory, and it breaks at the edges: the few orders that do go wrong, and the long waits when the rush is heavy. That's what the tool should target.

### What his notepad symbols mean
- **½** = half plate (no mark = full plate). *He confirmed this.*
- **Circled P** = parcel / takeaway. *He confirmed this.*
- **Bracket with a total** = one group's combined bill.

### Questions I still need to ask him
- What does a line struck through an order mean: served, paid, or both?
- Rows 1, 10 and 20 have circled numbers (①, ⑩). Are these table numbers? If so, he already partly uses a numbering system, and a token system would build on something he already does.

## Seen on earlier visits (my own experience, NOT in data.csv)
- The rush starts around 10 pm and lasts until about 1 am, and it's worst during exams, when students stay up all night.
- During the rush, orders often take 30–40 minutes, sometimes longer, and students get angry about the wait.
- Students order from the sitting area, and almost every order is customised: more spice, more salt, more sauce, no sweet corn, less sweet chai.
- I have seen two students order the same dish, and the first plate went to the student who ordered later.

## How data.csv was collected
- **Rows 1–21 (`source` = notepad_p1 / notepad_p2):** from my two photos of Kamal's notepad (17 Nov, visit 1). The first pass was transcribed with AI help from the photos; I then checked each row against the photos. `raw_text` is the line as he wrote it, and the other columns are my reading of it. Words I couldn't read are marked `?` instead of guessed. The pad has no times or names, so those columns are blank.
- **What this data can't show:** I watched the rush on only one night, and the notepad rows have no times. Wait times can't be measured properly from this data; my visit 2 experience (no order after 30 minutes) is one data point, not an average.
